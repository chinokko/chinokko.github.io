English

A reader and translation tool for iPhone and iPad. It recognises speech bubbles on your device and translates them with the engine you choose (on-device Apple translation or cloud engines).

chinokko is a browser, reader mode and translation tool. It does not host or distribute any content; the app ships without built-in sources, site lists or repository addresses. You add source packs yourself.

  • Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device.
  • No account is required; the developer has no server that collects data.
  • The free version is always available; the chinokko Pro subscription removes the limits and unlocks extra translation features.
